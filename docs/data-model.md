# Data Model Documentation

This document describes the data model for the LTI (Learning Tracking Initiative) application, including entity descriptions, field definitions, relationships, and an entity-relationship diagram.

# Model Descriptions

---

## AuditResult
Stores the overall result of an AI audit executed for a conversation. This entity acts as the aggregate root for all audit-related information.

**Fields:**

- `auditId`: Unique identifier for the audit (Primary Key, UUID)
- `chatId`: Identifier of the audited conversation
- `assistantId`: Identifier of the AI assistant that handled the conversation
- `status`: Current execution status of the audit
- `frustrationDetected`: Indicates whether customer frustration was detected during the conversation
- `lostLeadDetected`: Indicates whether a potential lost sales opportunity was detected
- `leadScore`: AI-generated score representing the quality of the lead
- `gapsCount`: Total number of knowledge gaps identified during the audit
- `errorMessage`: Error details when the audit execution fails
- `createdDate`: Timestamp when the audit record was created
- `auditedDate`: Timestamp when the audit process completed

**Validation Rules:**

- `auditId` is required and must be unique.
- `chatId` is required.
- `assistantId` is required.
- `status` is required and should belong to the audit status catalog.
- `frustrationDetected` must contain a boolean value.
- `lostLeadDetected` must contain a boolean value.
- `leadScore` should be between `0.000` and `1.000`.
- `gapsCount` must be greater than or equal to zero.
- `createdDate` is required.
- `auditedDate` cannot be earlier than `createdDate`.
- `errorMessage` is optional and should only contain information when the audit status is `FAILED`.

**Relationships:**

- `auditGaps`: One-to-many relationship with `AuditGap`
- `auditSentiments`: One-to-many relationship with `AuditSentiment`
- `auditRecommendations`: One-to-many relationship with `AuditRecommendation`

---

## AuditGap
Stores knowledge gaps detected during the audit process. Each record represents a customer question that the assistant was unable to answer adequately.

**Fields:**

- `gapId`: Unique identifier for the knowledge gap (Primary Key, UUID)
- `auditId`: Foreign key referencing the associated audit
- `messageId`: Identifier of the conversation message where the gap was detected
- `questionText`: Customer question identified as a knowledge gap
- `confidenceScore`: AI confidence score indicating certainty of the detected knowledge gap

**Validation Rules:**

- `gapId` is required and must be unique.
- `auditId` is required and must reference an existing `AuditResult`.
- `messageId` is required.
- `questionText` is required and cannot be empty.
- `confidenceScore` should be between `0.000` and `1.000`.

**Relationships:**

- `auditResult`: Many-to-one relationship with `AuditResult`

---

## AuditSentiment

Stores sentiment analysis results generated for each analyzed conversation message.

**Fields:**

- `sentimentId`: Unique identifier for the sentiment record (Primary Key, UUID)
- `auditId`: Foreign key referencing the associated audit
- `messageId`: Identifier of the analyzed conversation message
- `label`: Sentiment classification assigned by the AI model
- `score`: Confidence score of the sentiment prediction
- `sequenceOrder`: Sequential position of the message within the conversation

**Validation Rules:**

- `sentimentId` is required and must be unique.
- `auditId` is required and must reference an existing `AuditResult`.
- `messageId` is required.
- `label` is required and should belong to the sentiment catalog.
- `score` should be between `0.000` and `1.000`.
- `sequenceOrder` must be greater than zero.

**Relationships:**

- `auditResult`: Many-to-one relationship with `AuditResult`

---

## AuditRecommendation

Stores recommendations generated by the AI engine to improve future conversations or recover potential business opportunities.

**Fields:**

- `recommendationId`: Unique identifier for the recommendation (Primary Key, UUID)
- `auditId`: Foreign key referencing the associated audit
- `type`: Recommendation category
- `suggestedText`: AI-generated recommendation text
- `originMessageId`: Identifier of the conversation message that originated the recommendation

**Validation Rules:**

- `recommendationId` is required and must be unique.
- `auditId` is required and must reference an existing `AuditResult`.
- `type` is required and should belong to the recommendation type catalog.
- `suggestedText` is required and cannot be empty.
- `originMessageId` is optional.

**Relationships:**

- `auditResult`: Many-to-one relationship with `AuditResult`
